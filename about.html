Our Story

Building Wealth Through Smart Real Estate Investments

PineLeaf Estates was founded to close the trust gap in Nigerian land buying. Too many families lost money to unverified plots and unclear paperwork — we set out to change that, one verified title at a time.

Today, PineLeaf Estates specializes in land sales, estate development, building construction, property planning and management, documentation, land banking, and investment consultation, with every transaction anchored on verified, defensible titles across our operating states.
